## Active known failure: first checkbox on an unselected zone

The original zone scenario edits fields without a pointer selection, then unchecks
the enabled checkbox. It fails because the first click selects the zone and
restores checked=true before the change handler reads the native state. It is
retained **actively**, paired with the passing preselected-card control:
`zone canvas/field names ... (preselect=false/true)`.

Actual Chromium event trace (`zone-diagnostic.log`):

1. Click capture: `checked=false`, selected card `false`.
2. Click bubble: `checked=true`, selected card `true`.
3. Input and change capture/bubble: `checked=true`, selected card `true`.

The card's selection callback triggers a controlled rerender before the checkbox
change callback. Temporary diagnostic listeners were removed after capture.
`green-first.log` and `green-second.log` retain the original failures. A real
first-pointer and keyboard-first producer test is required by T7.168.1; no names-only
change here claims to fix or excuse this defect. The initial selected-card-only
13/13 GREEN and 76/76 related run are historical controls, **not final acceptance**.
