   exists. This is "not scanned", not "clean".

   (default-setup is `not-configured`).

7. There is no `dependabot.yml`. Actions, go2rtc Go modules, and Docker bases have no SCA except Trivy.
8. There is no CODEOWNERS file. Owners are recorded as `maintainers/<area>` placeholders.
