  slot. The regression creates an earlier empty slot and asserts exactly one

  count under the queue mutex. Shutdown waits on the condition variable until all
  workers finish, then closes/destroys resources. A delayed worker regression
  deliberately exceeds the old ten-second timeout and checks safe reinitialization.
