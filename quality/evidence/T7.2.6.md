DB handle or beginning its transaction. Every polygon must contain 3 through
`MAX_ZONE_POINTS` (32) points. The minimum matches `point_in_polygon()` in
`src/video/zone_filter.c`; one/two-point shapes cannot define a polygon. This
prevents the producer's count=33 read past the fixed point array and rejects
zero, negative and excessive counts before any persisted mutation. A zero-length
**zone list** still clears the stream's zones. Coordinate semantics and the
existing serialized-output overflow rollback are unchanged.
