Other locale catalogs retain the established English fallback for these new
keys; they were not padded with English copies or unreviewed translations.

responses, covering interpolation, quoted tags, singular/plural counts,
multiline camera/config examples, shortcuts and partial-locale fallback without
replacing already localized settings labels.

English fallback; this result does not claim these 14 strings were translated
into all supported languages. No baseline allowances changed.

Raw RED and catalog test output is in `build/eng-artifacts/T10/`.
