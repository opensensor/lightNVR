The correction contract allows preserving a preexisting temporary file while
succeeding through a distinct, exclusively created attempt artifact. This is
necessary for ordinary retries after a transient failure: the journal has no
persistent inode ownership proof authorizing truncation of an old partial file.
The new first producer will therefore retain its sentinel assertion and require
normal successful metadata/verified-byte publication from the distinct artifact.
This explicitly revised outcome does not change the older T7.26.3 rejection
contract. Unknown abandoned temporary entries must be retained and reported.

The implementation uses exclusive creation and at most eight attempts. An
occupied legacy name is preserved; subsequent names include a fresh UUID and
are also opened with O_EXCL/O_NOFOLLOW. Checked formatting rejects overlong
names. Retried jobs can progress without truncating prior partial bytes. The
abandoned filesystem cleanup scans job-associated attempt-name prefixes,
retains every candidate, logs its location, and leaves artifacts_cleaned false
with a persisted explanation/retry time. It does not treat a job-shaped name
as proof of ownership. Missing artifacts still complete cleanup normally.
